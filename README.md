## For Clients

Konvilo gives you a direct, private line to the professional of your choice.

- **Browse professionals** by specialty and find the right person for your needs
- **Send text and audio messages** at any time — no scheduling required
- **Buy message packages** tailored to how often you want to communicate (from a free trial to VIP plans)
- **Track your balance** per professional, right inside the conversation
- **Receive real-time replies** with push notifications so you never miss a response
- **All your messages are encrypted** — nobody else can read them, not even Konvilo

### How it works for clients

1. Create your account and browse available professionals
2. Claim your **free package** to send your first messages at no cost
3. If you want to continue, pick a paid package (Pro, Premium, or VIP) — each one adds messages and audio minutes to your balance
4. Start the conversation — text or voice, whichever is easiest for you

## For Professionals

Konvilo puts you in control of your time and your rates.

- **Set your own prices** per message and per audio minute — you decide what your time is worth
- **Choose your package tiers** (Free introductory, Pro, Premium, VIP) and what each one includes
- **Earn transparently** — track your earnings per client and per package from your dashboard
- **Team chats with colleagues** — create private group conversations with other professionals on the platform (free, no billing)
- **Manage your public profile** — specialty, bio, and package offerings visible to all clients
- **Work from any device** — Android, iOS, or the desktop app for Windows and macOS

### How it works for professionals

1. Register your account and complete your profile (specialty, bio, account code)
2. Set up your message packages and prices in the Packages settings
3. Clients discover your profile, buy a package, and start the conversation
4. You reply at your convenience — Konvilo handles the billing automatically

## Privacy & Security

Your conversations are private. Konvilo is built to keep them that way:

- **Encryption at rest** — all messages are encrypted using Google Cloud KMS. Neither Konvilo nor any third party can read the content of your conversations.
- **No data selling** — your personal information and conversations are never sold or shared with advertisers.
- **Account codes** — professionals are found by a unique 8-character account code, not by phone number or email. You control who can reach you.
- **Role-based access** — clients and professionals see only what is relevant to them.
